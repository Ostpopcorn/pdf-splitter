About PDF Splitter

PDF Splitter prints notes with pages that are longer than the paper, e.g., from a reMarkable. Each page is scaled to the width of the paper and split onto as many pages as it needs. The end of each page is repeated at the top of the next one, so that a line of handwriting that is cut at the end of a page is whole on the next page. The strokes stay sharp vectors, and each page is in the PDF only once, however often it is shown.
